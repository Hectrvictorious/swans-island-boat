Tickets

The state sells round-trip tickets online for this crossing. Both halves of a round trip work in either direction, and tickets aren't tied to a sailing, so buy ahead and keep them on your phone.

Buy Bass Harbor – Swan's Island tickets ›

Opens the Maine State Ferry Service webstore directly on this route. Download the PDF from the confirmation email before you get to the terminal: reception in the boarding lane is spotty, and the deckhand scans the barcode off your screen.

Rides on hand

A round trip is two rides. Tap what happened, and both phones keep the same count.

Fares

Tariff 11, effective June 1, 2025. Vehicles over 20 feet pay extra per foot. Truck tickets and all reservations are sold by phone or at the terminal, not online.

Taking a car

Only four car reservations and two truck reservations are sold per sailing, and the boat holds about 16 vehicles. A reservation is $17 each way on top of the vehicle fare, and it's the only way to be sure of a spot on a busy boat.

If your car is not reserved and you're leaving Swan's Island, the state asks you to call the Swan's Island terminal 24 hours before you plan to depart Bass Harbor, for line-up instructions.

Reservations are made by phone with the Bass Harbor terminal; the webstore doesn't sell them. Each day the island's Facebook group gets a "Traffic" post listing how many cars and how many feet of truck are reserved on every boat. This app reads that post every morning and evening and shows it on the schedule, so "Cars 4/4" means the car reservations on that boat are gone.

Which boat is it today?

Three boats take turns on this run. Each morning the app checks the boats' AIS signals to see which one is working the Bass Harbor run. If it's wrong, tap This one on the boat that's really running and both phones change.

Cancellations and notices

The state posts trip cancellations and service notices to New England 511; the app checks that feed about every half hour and shows anything posted for the Bass Harbor boats at the top of the Now tab. For same-minute news, the text alerts and the Facebook page are still the fastest.

Keep it on your phone

On Android, tap Install on the Now tab, or the browser menu (⋮) and Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Once it's installed it opens with no signal at the terminal, and it updates itself: when something new is added, the app refreshes on its own the next time it's opened. Nothing to reinstall.

Keep your ticket PDFs in Files or Photos too, in case the phone has no signal in the boarding lane.

About

Times come from the Maine State Ferry Service schedule pages: winter schedule effective November 1, 2026 through April 11, 2027, with the Frenchboro legs, the third-Tuesday fuel run, and the holiday pattern folded in. Summer 2027 uses the state's provisional table. Fares are Tariff 11.

Built for Josh and Sarah on Swan's Island, with a nod to Andy and Karter's swansislandferry.com, which started the idea of a schedule you can read on a phone. The ride rating is a rule of thumb from the forecast wind and seas, not a promise; the captain decides.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Maine State Ferry Service. Schedules change, boats break, and the weather has the final say. Confirm with the terminal before a trip that matters.
