Tickets

The state sells round-trip tickets online for this crossing. Both halves of a round trip work in either direction, and tickets aren't tied to a sailing, so buy ahead and keep them on your phone.

Fares

Tariff 11, effective June 1, 2025. Vehicles over 20 feet pay extra per foot. Truck tickets and all reservations are sold by phone or at the terminal, not online.

Taking a car

Only four car reservations and two truck reservations are sold per sailing, and the boat holds about 16 vehicles. A reservation is $17 each way on top of the vehicle fare, and it's the only way to be sure of a spot on a busy boat.

If your car is not reserved and you're leaving Swan's Island, the state asks you to call the Swan's Island terminal 24 hours before you plan to depart Bass Harbor, for line-up instructions.

Reservations don't exist online anywhere, not even on the state's own webstore, and the terminals don't publish how many spots are left on a given boat. The phone is the whole system.

Cancellations and notices

The state posts trip cancellations and service notices to New England 511; this page checks that feed three times a day and shows anything posted for the Bass Harbor boats. For same-minute news, the text alerts and the Facebook page are still the fastest.

Keep it on your phone

Tap the page name, Swan's Island Boat, at the top left of this page, then Add to Home Screen. That puts the boat on your home screen as its own app. (Installing from the browser's own menu gives you Claude instead, so use the page name.)

It needs a signal to open, so keep your ticket PDFs in Files or Photos too.

To share it, use the Share button at the top of this page and send the link; whoever gets it can add it to their phone the same way. Everyone sees the same schedule; the rides tally stays on each phone.

About

Times come from the Maine State Ferry Service schedule pages: winter schedule effective November 1, 2026 through April 11, 2027, with the Frenchboro legs, the third-Tuesday fuel run, and the holiday pattern folded in. Summer 2027 uses the state's provisional table. Fares are Tariff 11.

Built for Josh and Sarah on Swan's Island, with a nod to Andy and Karter's swansislandferry.com, which started the idea of a schedule you can read on a phone. The ride rating is a rule of thumb from the forecast wind and seas, not a promise; the captain decides.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by the Maine State Ferry Service. Schedules change, boats break, and the weather has the final say. Confirm with the terminal before a trip that matters.
